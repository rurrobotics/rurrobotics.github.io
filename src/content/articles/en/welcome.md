Welcome to the new home of RUR Robotics. We started in 2023 as a [Eurobot](https://www.eurobot.org/) team and have since grown into the robotics club of the School of Electrical Engineering at the University of Belgrade. The website should reflect that, so we rebuilt it.

## What we want the club to be

A place where anyone curious about robots can show up and start building, whatever their faculty, year or background. Robots bring together mechanics, electronics and software, so no one person knows all of it. We learn it from each other, and we want that to be easy to see from the outside too.

## Events and articles in one place

Everything the club does now lives in a single stream of articles. Each one has a category:

- **News:** announcements and updates from the club.
- **Writeup:** longer texts about what we built, how it works and what went wrong along the way.
- **Meetup** and **Lecture:** events. They also appear in the events section with their date and time, and move to past events once they are over.

On the [articles page](/en/articles/) you can search by title, filter by category and browse by year.

## Writing is open to everyone

Anyone in the club can write an article, whether it's a quick update or a deep dive into a project. Writing about what you built shouldn't take much more than explaining it to a teammate.

Every article is available in English, Serbian (Latin) and Serbian (Cyrillic), and the site follows the language you pick.

## What's next

You'll find meetups, lectures and writeups here as the season goes on, starting with the Eurobot 2027 rules and the Robotics Meet-up at Palata nauke. If you want to build robots with us, [send an application](/en/#join).
