On 9 November ETF Robotics is hosting the Robotics Meet-up, and we're taking part. Students, researchers and companies get together to talk robotics.

The program:

- **Student Robotics Showcase:** students present 10 of their projects.
- **ETF Robotics & Belgrade Robotics Hub:** research, lab capabilities and ways to work with industry.
- **Industry Spotlight:** FANUC Europe, HTEC, Microsoft, NOVELIC, Grid Dynamics, Toloka and Sunflower Labs talk about what they build and how young engineers can get involved.
- **Networking:** an informal chat after the talks.

**When:** 9 November 2026, 16:30–18:30, with a networking cocktail from 18:30.

**Where:** Palata nauke, Zadužbina Miodraga Kostića, Kralja Milana 11, 4th floor, Horizont hall, Belgrade.
