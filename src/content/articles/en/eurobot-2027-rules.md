[Eurobot](https://www.eurobot.org/) publishes the rules for the next season on Saturday, 19 September, and we're meeting in the lab to read them together.

Once we know what the table looks like, what the robots have to do and how many points each task is worth, we can start figuring out what to build. We'll go through the rules first and then talk about ideas and where to start.

We'll be in the lab at the School of Electrical Engineering.
