## 2. Data we process

- name, email address, phone number, faculty and year of study, as entered in the form;
- IP address and technical browser data, used solely to protect the form against abuse.

The website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools.

## 3. Purpose and legal basis

We process application data to review your application and to contact you regarding the selection process, as steps taken at your request prior to membership (Article 12(1)(2) of the Law). Technical data is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in protecting the form against abuse (Article 12(1)(6) of the Law).

Providing data is voluntary, but an application cannot be reviewed without it. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing.

## 4. Recipients

Data is accessed by the members of the association responsible for selection and by the following processors, acting solely on behalf of the association:

- Supabase – storage of applications (servers in the United Kingdom);
- Zoho Mail – sending emails (servers in the European Union);
- Cloudflare – website hosting and bot protection for the form.

Transfers to the United Kingdom and to European Union member states are made under Article 64 of the Law, as these countries provide an adequate level of protection. Any transfer to other countries is subject to standard contractual clauses (Article 65 of the Law), a copy of which may be requested at the email address above.

## 5. Retention

Applications are kept for 12 months from submission and are then deleted.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your data, the right to data portability and the right to object. Requests may be sent to [contact@rurrobotics.com](mailto:contact@rurrobotics.com), and we will respond within 30 days.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, 11000 Belgrade, [www.poverenik.rs](https://www.poverenik.rs).
